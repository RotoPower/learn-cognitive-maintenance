### What would change the verdict

- **Predict**: retrain with a cutoff after the CTF1 and GT1 onsets so more than one failure is in the training positives; drop or cap `hours_since_repair` (or normalise it within asset); re-score. A GO needs out-of-sample detection of at least 2 of 3 failures with lead time > 7 days and under about 0.2 false alerts per asset-month.
- **Anomaly**: the GT1 miss is structural: a 30-day rolling baseline tracks a 60-day linear drift. A fixed pre-fault reference baseline, load-normalised residuals, or a slope/CUSUM detector on CDP and EXH_TEMP would likely catch it and upgrade the verdict to unqualified.
- **Alert hygiene**: suppress or auto-close flags after a logged `corrective_repair` (reset the baseline at the repair timestamp). Removes both anomaly false alerts and 3 of the 7 predict false alerts.
